01 / About

At the intersection
of vision & graphics.

I study how we capture, represent and animate people in 3D.

At InterDigital’s Video Lab ↗, my research focuses on volumetric human representation, animation and standardization. My interests span multi-view performance capture, human–computer interaction and video-based character animation for the creative industries.

I completed my PhD at the Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing ↗ at the University of Surrey. My thesis, Learning to Animate Volumetric Video, explores learning-based methods for realistic character animation and real-time interaction in immersive environments.

Previously, I conducted postdoctoral research with Inria’s Morpheo and MimeTic teams. Before my PhD, I developed virtual reality simulation software for radiation therapy at Vertual Ltd, following an MSc in Games Programming at the University of Hull.
